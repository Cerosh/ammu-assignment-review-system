# Purpose

Capture lessons learned from the completed work.

The objective is continuous improvement rather than assigning blame.

Every retrospective should answer:

- What went well?
- What could have gone better?
- What should change?
- What should remain the same?

# Guiding Principle

A retrospective is an investment in future productivity.

The goal is not to judge past work but to improve future work.

Every retrospective should leave the project with clearer priorities, stronger engineering practices and a better development process than before.
