# Marking Criteria — Group Presentation

This rubric is deliberately incomplete for the "Effort" criterion, to test
that ambiguous/missing rubric information is not silently filled in.

| Criterion | Sound | High | Outstanding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research | Uses a small number of sources | Uses a range of relevant sources | Uses an extensive range of well-selected sources |
| Effort | - | - | Full marks awarded at teacher's discretion |
