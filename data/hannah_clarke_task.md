# Task Instructions

You are required to complete both Parts A & B of this oral task on any ONE area of the Australian Law below:

- Family Law, Criminal Law, Consumer Law, Workplace Law, Environmental Law

## PART A: GROUP TASK — Media File

1. Collect and analyse 5-6 media articles on your chosen area of law and for each article, highlight:
   - The source and name of the article
   - The legal terms and concepts
2. Annotate the meanings of the legal terms and concepts in the article.
3. Discuss each of the cases in your groups.
4. Allocate each case to a group member to present in class.

## PART B: ORAL AND VISUAL PRESENTATION

Include in your presentation:

1. A summary of the case (Minimum 500 words).
2. Explain the outcome of the case.
3. Analyse whether justice was served in the case.
