The Industrial Revolution happened in Britain because of a few big reasons. One reason was new machines like the steam engine which made factories possible. Another reason was that lots of people moved from the countryside to cities to find work in the new factories. A third reason was that Britain had lots of coal and iron which the factories needed.

The Industrial Revolution changed a lot of things for ordinary people. Workers had to work very long hours in factories, sometimes 12 or 14 hours a day, and conditions were often unsafe. Cities got very crowded and dirty because so many people moved there so fast. But some people also got new jobs and wages that they didn't have before when they were farmers.

I think the social consequence was the biggest one for ordinary people because factory conditions were so bad. Many workers, including children, worked in dangerous conditions. This shows that the Industrial Revolution had a big effect on how people lived their everyday lives, even though it also made Britain richer overall.

In conclusion, the Industrial Revolution was caused by new inventions, people moving to cities, and Britain's natural resources. It had both good and bad effects on ordinary people's lives, but the working conditions were the most significant social consequence.
