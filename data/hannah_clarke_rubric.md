# Marking Criteria

| Outcome | Outstanding (5 marks) | High (4 marks) | Sound (3 marks) | Basic (2 marks) | Limited (1 mark) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **5.3** Examines the role of law in society. | Student clearly identifies the Australian Law, the source, legal terms and concepts. Student provides a detailed description of their chosen case. Student clearly identifies and links the summary to outcome and justification in written form. | Student identifies the Australian Law, the source, legal terms and concepts. Student provides a description of their chosen case. Student identifies and links the summary, outcome and justification in written form. | Student soundly identifies the Australian Law, the source, legal terms and concepts. Student provides a sound description of their chosen case. Student identifies and soundly links the summary, outcome and justification in written form. | Student provides a brief description of their chosen case. Student identifies and satisfactorily links the summary, outcome and justification in written form. | Student provides a vague description of their chosen case. Student provides a limited description of their chosen case. |
| **5.8** Explains information using a variety of forms. | Student presents an intensively researched assessment task in class. Student has researched widely using a broad range of media. Student demonstrates an outstanding understanding of the Australian Law. | Student presents a thorough researched assessment task in class. Student has researched widely using a range of media. Student demonstrates a high level of understanding of the Australian Law. | Student presents a sound researched assessment task in class. Student has researched from a limited number of sources. Student demonstrates a sound level of understanding of the Australian Law. | Student presents a satisfactorily researched assessment task in class. Student has researched from a small number of sources. Student demonstrates a basic level of understanding of the Australian Law. | Very limited evidence of research presented in class except having some information. |
| **5.9** Works independently and collaboratively to meet individual and collective goals within specified timeframes. | Student provides an extensive analysis of their chosen legal case. Student provides a detailed summary of the case. Student provides a detailed explanation on the area of law. Student provides an extensive discussion on the outcome of the case. Student provides a wide range of reasons to justify their arguments on the case. | Student provides a detailed discussion of their chosen legal case. Student provides a detailed summary of the case. Student provides an explanation on the area of law. Student provides a discussion on the outcome of the case. Student provides reasons to justify their arguments on the case. | Student provides a sound description of their chosen legal case. Student provides a brief summary of the case. Student provides a description of the area of law. Student provides a description on the outcome of the case. Student provides a few reasons to justify their arguments on the case. | Student provides a brief description of their chosen legal case. Student provides a brief summary of the case. Student states the area of law. Student provides a brief description on the outcome of the case. Student provides a reason to justify their argument on the case. | Student provides vague description of their chosen legal case. Student may state the area of law. Student may state the outcome of the case. Student may state a reason about the case. |
