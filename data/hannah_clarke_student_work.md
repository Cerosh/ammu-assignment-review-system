The Hannah Clarke Domestic Violence Murder Case (2020)
After a dangerously terrible accident, Hannah Clark was able to give a detailed description of the events that lead up to the accident that had killed herself ,her three children and her ex-husband.

A woman named Hannah Clark, got married to a guy named Rowan Baxter. Baxter was a controlling husband, he controlled everything in her life, like what she could wear, to when she could meet her family and friends. On February 19 2020, Hannah Clark and her 3 children died. This case included four victims: Hannah Clarke (33), Aaliyah Baxter (6), Laianah Baxter (4) and Trey Baxter (3). Ever since the murder, Mr Clarke and Mrs Clarke always had one question "Why?" Why did Rowan Baxter murder their only daughter and their three grandchildren?

Baxter and Clarke first met at the PCYC in 2008. While Baxter was living with his current wife and his son, he continued to have a relationship with Hannah, later on the relationship with Hannah, Baxter was open to getting divorced with his wife. Later on the relationship Rowen and Hannah moved in with a friend, six months into Clarke and Baxter's relationship Hannah found out that she was pregnant, Rowan Baxter forced her to abort the child because she was "too young" at that time she was 23 years old, as Baxter told her, Hannah aborted her first child. This action revealed signs of coercive control and domestic violence.

On 19 October 2012, Kingscliff Rowen Baxter and Hannah Clarke got married, at that time Hannah was 14 weeks pregnant with Aaliyah. Mr.Clarke and Mrs.Clarke managed the finances of Rowen and Hannah's wedding. Baxter was furious that the guest who attended the wedding didn't pay enough money to them, as Baxter wanted to use the money to have a Honeymoon with his wife. Aaliyah Baxter was born on 10 April 2013. After the newlyweds first child was born, Baxter refused to pay his ex-wife any child support for their son.

In late 2014 in a suburb in Melbourne, Capalaba, Baxter and Hannah opened a gym. Their gym wasn't doing well due to Baxter's toxic comments about women and calling them "pigs" and having severe financial problems to keep the gym running. Causing the gym to shut down. Baxter would often leave the bills in the Clarke family's mail box, making them close most of the bills for Rowen and Hannah.

In January 2015 Rowen opened another gym called "Intergr8" at Mansfield and later Laianah was born in March 2015 and Trey was born in December 2016. Once again "Intergr8" was not doing well due to Baxter being a poor businessman, later he moved his gym back to Capalaba. But he had to move from there due to Council zoning issues.

The history of domestic violence and coercive control began after Hannah and Baxter's relationship was formed. Shortly after Hannah and Baxter began living together. Baxter set up strong rules for Hannah, like she was not allowed to wear shorts, short skirts or the colour pink. Baxter shared a facebook page with Hannah, and did not allow Hannah to have her own.
Further into the relationship, Baxter refused to allow Hannah to spend time with her family, this occurred on several occasions. Baxter even criticised Mrs Clarke in front of Hannah and her brother, he even didn't allow Hannah to spend time with her mother on mothers day. As they were further into their relationship Baxter would begin to be more distant from his wife, his wife's family and his own children. Whenever Hannah didn't do what Baxter asked for, he would go days without talking to her, another incident that occurred was when the kids didn't put their toys away they would throw them away. All these actions which included him getting angry quickly to him staying silent all showed signs of coercive control. Baxter's controlling behaviour began to escalate in November 2019.

In the lead up to the murder of Hannah Clarke and her three children, Hannah Clarke separated from Rowan Baxter and had a DVO (Domestic Violence Order). During this time Rowen Baxter abducted his 4 year old daughter when Hannah Clarke did not allow him to have the children for the night. Baxter kept on having back and forth conversations with Hannah regarding the children, when Hannah got the DVO she changed her number and her phone, because she suspected that Baxter placed a hearing device in her phone and a tracking device. Oftentimes Baxter would cry about not staying with the kids and would often call and text Hannah in her old phone asking to talk and to take her out.

On February 19 2020, Rowan Baxter, stopped Hannah Clark car, inside were her 3 children, during that time Baxter poured petrol on the car, and lit it up before killing himself. Whilst Baxter received only 80% of burns on his body, Hannah received 97% of burns, only the sole of her feet were left unburned. The Clark's family chose Hannah's footprint as a memorabilia.
This headline attracted the attention of everyone, this non-physical domestic abuse, which is also known as coercive control or "intimate terrorism" was pushed to become a crime in Australia. In Queensland on May 26, in the memory of Hannah Clark and her 3 children. A law was created named the "Hannah Law", this law is focused to help any one who is going through coercive control, those who cause physical, emotional or mental harm could face up to 14 years in prison.
The legal concepts that were present during this case included; Coercive control, Domestic violence orders (DVO's) and breach of orders. The Queensland court records reveal all the legal concepts and how they were present in the Hannah Clarke murder case. In the record, it was shown that Baxter had breached the Domestic Violence order numerous times, that the police took him in, but later let him. It was also shown how Baxter had coercive control over Hannah and her kids, which became more serious from November 2019. All these shown on the court record revealed that the police did not respond actively to all the signs of coercive control stated by Senior Constable Kristen Kent, who was the local domestic violence liaison officer, who worked closely with Hannah who provided details on what happened between Baxter and herself. In the ABC news article Ms.Brasch presented a series of ideas to Coroner Jane Bentley after the Hannah Clarke case, and one of the ideas was providing better training to the responder of domestic violence which included a 5 day specialist training course and a refresher training to be taken annually.
At the end of the Hannah Clarke case justice was not served, since the perpetrator (Rowen Baxter) had died at the scene by suicide with a knife through his chest. Instead Mr.Clarke and Mrs.Clarke created a charity named "Small Steps 4 Hannah" this charity continued Hannah's legacy whilst having huge impact on people, this provided the people the training needed to understand coercive control.
