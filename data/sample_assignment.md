# Assignment: The Industrial Revolution — Causes and Consequences

**Subject:** History
**Year Level:** 9
**Word limit:** 800–1000 words
**Due date:** Friday, Week 8

## Task

Analyse the causes of the Industrial Revolution in Britain and evaluate its
most significant social and economic consequences.

## Requirements

Your response must:

- Explain at least THREE causes of the Industrial Revolution.
- Use evidence from at least TWO primary or secondary sources.
- Evaluate which consequence (social or economic) had the greatest impact on
  ordinary people, and justify your judgement.
- Include a clear introduction and conclusion.

## Format

- Written response, 800–1000 words.
- Reference list in Chicago style.
