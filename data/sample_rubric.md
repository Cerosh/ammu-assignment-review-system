# Marking Criteria — The Industrial Revolution

| Criterion | Limited | Basic | Sound | High | Outstanding |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Knowledge & Understanding | Identifies one cause with little detail | Identifies causes but with gaps | Explains causes accurately | Explains causes with good detail | Demonstrates deep understanding of causes and consequences |
| Use of Evidence | Little or no evidence used | Evidence used but not clearly linked to claims | Evidence supports most claims | Evidence is well-selected and clearly linked | Uses well-selected, relevant evidence to support every claim |
| Analysis | Mostly describes events | Some explanation of relationships | Explains relationships between causes and consequences | Explains relationships with nuance | Explains relationships between causes and consequences, not just listing them |
| Evaluation | No judgement made | Judgement made without justification | Judgement made with some justification | Judgement clearly justified | Makes and justifies a clear judgement about significance |
| Communication | Unclear structure, frequent errors | Structure present but inconsistent | Clear structure, accurate referencing | Well-structured, accurate referencing | Writes a clear, well-structured response with accurate referencing |
