# Marking Criteria — Causes and Consequences of the Industrial Revolution

This rubric does not use outcome codes -- only named criteria.

- **Historical Explanation**: How well the causes and consequences of the Industrial Revolution are explained, including cause-and-effect relationships.
- **Source Use**: How effectively evidence from primary and secondary sources is integrated and referenced.
- **Judgement**: How clearly a reasoned judgement is made about which consequence mattered most, and how well it is justified.
- **Written Expression**: Clarity, structure and accuracy of the written response, including referencing style.
